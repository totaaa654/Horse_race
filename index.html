01 About the game

Small horses.
Big energy.

Gallop Night is a quick, colorful horse race built for the people already in the room.

No accounts. No complicated rules. Everyone picks a unique horse, the race begins, and the whole family watches the track unfold together.

02 How it works

From the stable
to the finish.

Three simple steps. One unpredictable winner.

  1. 01

    Choose the field

    Decide how many horses will race, from a quick two-horse dash to a full eight-lane showdown.

  2. 02

    Claim your colors

    Every player picks a unique pixel racer. Once a color is claimed, it belongs to that player.

  3. 03

    Watch and cheer

    The countryside rolls by, the horses trade places, and everybody finds out who takes the crown.
